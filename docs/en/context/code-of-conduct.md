# CANN Open Project Code of Conduct

1. To build an open and friendly community environment, contributors and maintainers of this community pledge to:
   Ensure that participants in this community are free from any harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, experience level, education level, social status, nationality, personal appearance, race, or other characteristics.

2. Behaviors that contribute to creating a positive community environment include but are not limited to:

   - Using friendly and inclusive language.
   - Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
   - Patiently accepting constructive criticism.
   - Focusing on what is best for the community.
   - Being kind to other community members.

3. Behaviors that participants in this community should not engage in include but are not limited to:

   - Posting statements or images related to pornography, violence, or similar content.
   - Trolling, inciting, or rumor-mongering behavior; insulting or derogatory comments; personal and political attacks.
   - Harassing other participants of this community, whether publicly or privately.
   - Publishing personal information of others, such as home addresses and email addresses, without explicit authorization.
   - Other inappropriate behavior reasonably deemed to violate the community code of conduct.

4. Community project maintainers have the right and obligation to interpret what constitutes "inappropriate behavior" and to properly and fairly correct inappropriate behavior that has occurred. Community project maintainers have the right and obligation to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and any other contributions that violate this code of conduct; community project maintainers may temporarily or permanently ban any participant whose behavior they deem threatening, offensive, or harmful to community order.
